Merged state reviewed: `ddc3d51` (Merge v2 V8-C). Read-only: no app code was changed.

## How this was checked

- `npm run web-smoke -- --dev`: **passed**, ko · ja · en, 249 screenshots, no page errors.

  - the 5 moods × 4 paper patterns;

  - ko A4 chart;

  - ko A4 areas, blank.
- The widgets and the Live Activity cannot run on the web. They were reviewed by reading `src/widgets/*.tsx`. The notification, iCloud and SyncChoiceSheet rows are iOS-only. On the web they show only their ruled notice, so they were reviewed from code.
- Contrast ratios were computed with `src/theme/moods.ts` (`MOODS`, `withAccent`, `contrastEdition`), `palette.ts` (`contrast`) and `chart.ts` (`legibleMark`). Every mood was checked with the gold, rouge and indigo accents. The script is reproduced at the end.

Screenshots are in `docs/v2/review/R-6/`. They are referenced below as `R-6/<file>`.

## Verdict

**Design direction (R-6): passes.**
- No new surface uses a card, badge, pill, gradient, sparkle, emoji, crown, lock, flower or stamp.
- Pro is marked with a plain word ("프로" / "Pro"), never a lock or a crown.
- The ProSheet is a numbered ruled list with one button.
- YearClose has no celebration. The Year review is a ruled ledger page with a double rule.
- The PDF is a printed table on white with hairline rules.
- The store screenshots are plain: a caption over an unframed app capture, with no device frame, gradient or sticker.
- What remains is polish (all P2). The one P1 in this section is a print defect in the PDF area pages (D-1).

**Accessibility (R-7): three P1s.**
- A-1: composed VoiceOver labels on the new surfaces carry no `accessibilityLanguage`.
- A-2: VoiceOver order across the two pages of the spread is not guaranteed.
- A-3: two marks fall below the contrast floor:
  - the YearLedger "recorded day" mark is only a hairline;
  - the widget's done-row text is about 2.3:1.

## R-6 Design direction

### D-1 · P1 · PDF area pages: the row rules break under the Action column
- **Evidence:** `R-6/pdf-en-Letter-area1.png`. Under each action row, the rule below the Action cell is offset from the rule under the Type and day cells by about 1px and ends at the column edge. Printed, it looks like a broken ledger line on every one of the 8 rows on all 8 pages.
- **Cause:** `src/lib/printSheet.ts:348` sets `.rows td.act{display:flex;…}`. A `display:flex` `<td>` stops being a table cell, so its border no longer collapses with the row.
- **Fix:**
  1. Keep the `<td>` a table cell and put the flex box inside it. In `areaPage` (`printSheet.ts:269`), render the cell as:
     `<td class="act"><div class="ai">${check}<span>${escLines(text)}</span></div></td>`
  2. Replace line 348 with:
     `.rows td.act{vertical-align:middle}` and `.rows .ai{display:flex;align-items:center;gap:${s(5)}}`
  3. Change the line-clamp selector on line 349 to `.rows .ai span`.

### D-2 · P2 · PDF: the day numbers and A5 cell text are too small to read in print
- **Evidence:**
  - `printSheet.ts:352` `.rows th.d{font-size:${s(5.5)}}`: this is 5.5pt on A4 and Letter (k≈1.0), and about 3.9pt on A5 (k≈0.706).
  - `.t81 .in` is 8.5pt × k, which is about 6.0pt on A5. See `R-6/pdf-ja-A5-chart.png`.
- **Fix:**
  - In `printSheet.ts:352`, use `font-size:${pt(Math.max(5.5, 6.5 * k))}`.
  - In `printSheet.ts:323`, use `font-size:${pt(Math.max(6.5, 8.5 * k))}`.
  - Apply the same floor to `.t81 .head .in,.t81 .core .in` on line 324: `Math.max(7, 9 * k)`.

### D-3 · P2 · PDF chart page: the header and memo do not share the chart's left edge
- **Evidence:** `R-6/pdf-ko-A4-chart.png`. "목표 달성표", the goal and the memo rules start at the page padding. The chart is 470k wide and centred inside 515k, so it is inset about 22pt. On a printed page this reads as two different margins.
- **Fix:** In `chartPage` (`printSheet.ts:186`), use `const side = 515 * f.k`. Then check that the page still fits, since there is 33pt of slack below the five memo rules on A4. Otherwise, wrap the head, goal, sum and memo in `<div style="width:${pt(side)}">`.

### D-4 · P2 · Paper patterns: grid and lines are as strong as the content rules
- **Evidence:** `R-6/patterns-moods.png` (every mood × dots, grid, lines, blank) and `R-6/patterns-zoom.png`.
  - The pattern stroke `rgba(60,45,20,0.11)` at 0.5pt has the same weight as the ruled-list dividers (`M.line`, 0.10–0.12 at 1px). That makes about 1.2:1 for both.
  - The pattern lines fall on the 22pt dot rhythm, not on the 52pt row rhythm, so the list reads double-ruled.
  - Dark mode is fine. Dots and blank are fine.
- **Fix:** In `src/components/PaperDots.tsx:43`, lower the alpha for lines only, and keep dots as they are:
  ```ts
  const a = pattern === 'dots' ? (M.dark ? (more ? 0.15 : 0.08) : (more ? 0.2 : 0.11))
                               : (M.dark ? (more ? 0.10 : 0.05) : (more ? 0.12 : 0.065));
  ```
  Use `rgba(60,45,20,a)` for light and `rgba(255,255,255,a)` for dark. Apply the same to the widget: in `TodayWidget.ios.tsx:73`, use `opacity(0.55)` on the pattern rule rectangles.

### D-5 · P2 · Settings › paper: the pattern swatches are barely visible
- **Evidence:** `R-6/ko-paper-locked.png`. In `PaperRows.tsx:21`, the swatch ink is `M.faint`. Its contrast on the paper is:

  | Mood | Contrast |
  |---|---|
  | 연꽃 | 2.46 |
  | 크림 | 2.36 |

- **Fix:** `const ink = legibleMark(M.faint, M.bg, M);` (import from `../../theme/chart`). This gives 3.0:1 in every mood.

### D-6 · P2 · The "Pro" word is styled two ways
- **Evidence:**
  - `YearShelf.tsx:144` and the `LockedLine` / `LockedSheet` rows use `M.ink` at weight 700.

## R-7 Accessibility

### A-1 · P1 · Composed VoiceOver labels on the new surfaces have no `accessibilityLanguage`
- **Evidence:** `AppText.tsx:154–186` and `Pressy.tsx:30` add `accessibilityLanguage={a11yLang(lang())}`. But an `accessible` `View` or `Pressable` with its own `accessibilityLabel` is read with *its own* language. When the app language differs from the device language (ja app on a ko iPhone), VoiceOver reads these labels with the device voice. The affected elements:
  - `LineRow` (`common.tsx:811`, `:817`): every Settings row, PrintSheet, YearRows and ProSection;
  - `PaperRows.tsx:77`;
  - `LanguageSection.tsx:60` (it sets the language only on the per-language rows, not on "system");
  - `YearShelf.tsx:87`, `:118`;
  - `YearSheet.tsx` `AreaRow` and `SheetLink`;
  - `YearReview.tsx`: the `Numbers` cells, `Frames`, `WeekNotes`, `Reflections`, and the links at 152 and 372;
  - `YearLedger.tsx:67`;
  - `YearClose.tsx`: `LedgerRow` (196), the mode radios (269), the close button (148);
  - `ProSheet.tsx:141` (feature rows);
  - `SyncChoiceSheet.tsx:86`, `:151`;
  - `RemindRow.tsx:54` (adjustable).
- **Fix:** Add one helper in `src/components/AppText.tsx`:
  ```ts
  /** 화면 읽기 말의 언어 — 라벨을 직접 단 View · Pressable에 펼쳐 넣는다 */
  export const a11yLangProp = () => ({ accessibilityLanguage: a11yLang(lang()) });
  ```
  Spread `{...a11yLangProp()}` on each element listed above. In `LineRow`, put it on both the `View` and the `Pressable` branch; that alone covers about 60% of the list. For `LanguageSection.tsx:67`, keep the per-row override and fall back to `a11yLang(lang())` for "system".
- **Test:** In `tests/a11y-lang.test.js`, source-assert that every `accessibilityLabel=` in `src/{screens/year,screens/settings,overlays/{ProSheet,PrintSheet,YearClose},components/{YearLedger,RemindRow,common}}` sits on an element with `accessibilityLanguage` or `a11yLangProp`.

### A-2 · P1 · Spread: the VoiceOver order across the two pages is not guaranteed
- **Evidence:** `Root.tsx:494–520`.
  - The left page, binding, right page and tabs are plain sibling `View`s. iOS VoiceOver sorts non-container siblings by frame (top-to-bottom, then left-to-right), so rows on the left page and the right page at the same height can interleave. For example: the Home "오늘 할 일" row, then the 81-cell row 4, then the next Home row.
  - `docs/v2/V5-A.md:37` asserts "VoiceOver order follows the tree", but nothing verifies it.
  - On the web, DOM order is correct.
- **Fix:** RN 0.85 ships `experimental_accessibilityOrder` (`node_modules/react-native/Libraries/Components/View/ViewPropTypes.js`).
  1. Give the pages `nativeID="spread-left"` (`Root.tsx:499`) and `nativeID="spread-right"` (`:506`).
  2. Give the tab-bar wrapper `nativeID="spread-tabs"`.
  3. On the host `View` at `:494`, set `experimental_accessibilityOrder={['spread-left', 'spread-right', 'spread-tabs']}`.
  4. Add a device-QA line to `docs/v2/device-tests.md`: "iPad 13" landscape, VoiceOver: swipe right from the Home title reaches every Home row before any 81-cell cell."

## Contrast script (reproducible)

`node <script>` from the repo root. It prints, per mood and accent:
- the paper mark (standard and Increase Contrast);
- the swatch ink;
- the line;
- `sub` on the paper and on the sheet;
- the ledger fills at 30/50/70/90%;
- the ledger edge colour and its ratio;
- the widget done text;
- wear level 1.
