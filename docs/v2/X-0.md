## Translation choices

- **Glossary.** I followed `src/i18n/glossary.ts` and plan §2.6, which differ from the shorter list in the session prompt:
  - 실천 → 行動 / action;
  - 영역 → 分野 / area;
  - 칸 → マス / square;
  - 했어요 → できた / done;
  - 달성 → 達成 / achieved;
  - 오늘 할 일 → 今日やること / Today's list.

  Where the prompt gave エリア, 実践 or cell, the checked-in glossary and the lint (`BANNED_EN` bans "cell") decide.
- **Tab bar.**
  - ja: ホーム・マンダラート・入れる・今日・統計.
  - en: Home · Chart · Add · Today · Stats.
  - Notes:
    - 담기 is 「今日に入れる」 in the glossary, but that is too long for an 11 pt tab label, so the tab says 入れる and the hint carries 今日やること.
    - "Chart" follows the glossary's "the chart (Mandalart)".
  - Checked at 390 px: nothing truncates.
- **Spoken strings.**
  - The week strip reads full day names in ja and en:
    - en: "Monday: done, Tuesday: missed…";
    - ja: 「月曜日 できた、火曜日 できなかった…」.

    Only ko keeps the one-syllable 월 함 form.
  - List separators come from `core.listSep` (ko and en `, `, ja `、`).
  - The ordinal cell is `fmt.ordinalCell`: "third square" / 3番目のマス.
- **Area summary.** This is one function with all parameters, so word order is natural in each language:
  - en: "Health, 3 of 8 squares achieved, 2 on Today's list";
  - ja: 健康、8マス中3マス達成、今日2マス.
- **Area 8/8 toast.** The fallback is the area number when there is no title:
  - en: "Area 3: all 8 squares achieved";
  - ja: 分野3の8マスをすべて達成しました;
  - ja with a title: 「健康」の8マスをすべて達成しました.
- **Display names.**
  - Paper:
    - ja: アイボリー・クリーム・グレー・藤色・ダーク;
    - en: Ivory · Cream · Gray · Lavender · Dark.
  - Corners:
    - ja: 丸く・四角く;
    - en: Rounded / Sharp. "Square" is avoided because it is the glossary word for 칸.
  - Mood:
    - ja: つらい・いまいち・ふつう・よい・最高;
    - en: Rough · Meh · Okay · Good · Great.
  - Accents: these are traditional dye names in ja (ゴールド・臙脂・よもぎ・藍・桑の実) and plain color names in en (Gold · Rouge · Sage · Indigo · Mulberry). In en, 쑥 is "Sage" rather than "Mugwort", which few readers would picture.
- **Repeat labels.**
  - ja:
    - 「月曜日に繰り返す」;
    - 「繰り返し：平日」. The full-width colon avoids the wrong particle after 毎日.
  - en:
    - "Repeat on Mondays";
    - "Repeat: Weekdays".
- **Plurals and counters.** `labelCount` and `fitLines` use `plural()` in en. ja uses 件 for the index-tab count.

## Deviations

- **`ORDINAL` is a live list over a new `core.ordinal({ n })`**, not `fmt.ordinalCell`.
  - Why: `tests/i18n-catalog.test.js` (W0, not mine) still builds `${ORDINAL[ai]} 번째 칸`, and four files owned by other packages still import `ORDINAL`.
  - Result: in ko it is unchanged. In ja and en it gives 1番目 / first.
- **`DOW_KR` and `WEEKDAY_KR` follow the language**, as plan §6 asks (liveList over `dowNarrow`).
  - Side effect: until their owners extract them, other packages' templates that append Korean (`${DOW_KR[d]}요일`) render mixed text in ja/en, for example "W요일" on Home in en.
  - The same applies to `ORDINAL` inside Korean templates in areaPage, sheetSpeech, ActionSheet and FullGrid.
  - This is the expected mid-migration state. ko is byte-identical.
- **One token in `src/components/WeekStrip.tsx` (owned by X-H, wave 7): `key={d}` → `key={i}` in `WeekCaption`.**
  - Why: with `WEEKDAY_KR` following the language, the en narrow letters repeat (M T W T F S S). React then logged "two children with the same key" in en, which fails `web-smoke --dev`.
  - No wave-6 package owns this file. X-H should keep index keys when it extracts the file.
- **`ACCENTS[].name` is a getter, not a `liveList`.** This keeps the mutable-array type and object identity that Settings and six tests rely on.

## Handoffs

- **X-H (Home), `src/screens/Dashboard.tsx`**
  - `{DOW_KR[now.getDay()]}요일` should become `fmt.dowLong`.
  - The week chart's `${DOW_KR[d.dow]}요일 ${d.count}번` needs the same change.
- **X-F (Stats), `src/lib/statsView.ts` and `src/components/StatsCharts.tsx`**
  - These use `DOW_KR` and `MOOD_WORDS` inside Korean templates.
  - Prefer `fmt.dowShort` or `fmt.dowLong` and `core.mood0`–`core.mood4`.
- **X-C (Sheet), `src/lib/sheetSpeech.ts` and `src/overlays/ActionSheet.tsx`, and X-E (Chart), `src/lib/areaPage.ts` and `src/screens/FullGrid.tsx`**
  - These use `ORDINAL[…]` with Korean 번째 / 번째 칸 suffixes.
  - Use `fmt.ordinalCell({ n })` for the cell position. For "8개 영역 중 N 번째", add your own key that takes `{ n }`.
  - After that, `ORDINAL` only serves the W0 catalog test, and V8-A can remove it.
- **X-C (Sheet), `src/lib/actionSheet.ts`**: uses `DOW_KR` for a day letter.
- **X-B (Kinds) or X-H (Home): long en day lists.**
  - en `daysLabel` writes "Mon, Tue, Wed, Thu, Sun". On the Home and Today rows, together with the area name and the Korean suffix 반복, it truncates the area name at 390 px (`en-home.png`).
  - When the kind line is extracted, consider putting the schedule before the area, or shortening the area name first.
- **X-G (Settings)**: `Settings.tsx` builds `종이 ${MOOD_LABEL[mood]}` and `칸 모서리 ${SHAPE_LABEL[shape]}`.
  - Read `core.paper*` and `core.shape*` directly, or keep the live views.
  - Accent names come from `ACCENTS[i].name`, which is already translated.
- **X-D (Today)**: `Today.tsx` passes `MGMT_LABEL` to `PureTextTabs`. That keeps working as a live record. Reading `core.mgmt*` directly is also fine.
- **V8-A**: can remove the `@deprecated` live views once nobody imports them:
  - `DOW_KR`, `WEEKDAY_KR` and `ORDINAL`;
  - `MGMT_LABEL`, `SHAPE_LABEL`, `MOOD_LABEL` and `MOOD_WORDS`.

## For a native speaker to double-check

- **ja**
  - 入れる alone as the tab label for 담기. Is it clear without 今日に?
  - The paper and corner names, especially 丸く / 四角く, which are adverbial. 丸 / 角 or 丸型 / 角型 may read better in a settings list.
  - 藤色 for 연보라.
  - Mood words つらい・いまいち・ふつう・よい・最高.
  - The crash-screen wording 「記録を書き出す」. Settings may later use エクスポート; keep the two consistent.
  - 「今日は休み」 and 「できなかった」 in the spoken week strip.
- **en**
  - "Sage" for 쑥, and "Rouge" for 연지.
  - "Sharp" as the opposite of "Rounded" for cell corners.
  - "Meh" in the mood row: it may be too casual.
  - "Add" for 담기, versus "Add to Today".
  - The spoken week strip pattern "Monday: done".
  - "Something changed in the meantime, so this can't be undone".
